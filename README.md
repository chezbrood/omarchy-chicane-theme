# Chicane

An [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org) theme in Rocoto, one palette of graphite ground and a single rocoto red, with
nine backgrounds of race week in Las Vegas made from code and real map data (and a tenth, plain one), in order: arriving, the desert at sunset, the circuit, the tyre in pieces, lights out, fireworks off the rooftops, the podium, the pit lane the morning after, and a chicane and its sound to end on. Every background opens with its own intro, drawn frame by frame, that ends exactly on the still. No words anywhere.

Its light twin is [omarchy-chicane-light-theme](https://github.com/chezbrood/omarchy-chicane-light-theme). The other chapter, in the same colours, is [omarchy-rocoto-theme](https://github.com/chezbrood/omarchy-rocoto-theme).

## Install

```
omarchy theme install https://github.com/chezbrood/omarchy-chicane-theme
```

The download is about 70 MB. Then pick it under Style > Theme, and step through the backgrounds with
`Super + Ctrl + W`. Every background plays its intro when you select it, and at login.

## Backgrounds

| # | Background | Scene |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | `01-vegas-globe` | The same planet turns to rest on Las Vegas |
| 02 | `02-red-rock` | Red Rock Canyon's escarpment in contour lines, the sun setting behind it |
| 03 | `03-strip-circuit` | The Las Vegas Strip Circuit in isometric hairline; the Sphere plays the globe, then powers on as a helmet |
| 04 | `04-tyre` | A tyre taken apart along its axle, an exploded parts drawing, the three compounds floating off the end |
| 05 | `05-silver-arrow` | Lights out: a car launching off the grid at night, engraved in line screen |
| 06 | `06-encore-rooftops` | The Strip from the air after the race, fireworks off the hotel roofs, in dither |
| 07 | `07-encore-podium` | The podium after the ceremony in halftone, confetti down, the Bellagio fountains up |
| 08 | `08-pit-lane` | The pit building from overhead the morning after, its roof still lit from the concert |
| 09 | `09-chicane` | The break: neon lines racing through a chicane, the red car's engine standing over it as ridgelines of its sound |
| 10 | `10-unlock` | The unlock screen's wordmark in rocoto red, on its own: the plain one |

## Made with

Each background is drawn in code: a web page that can draw any moment of its intro, rendered frame by frame. Credits for the map and terrain data are in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
